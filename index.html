Early AM
Oxbow Bend for calm water, mountain reflections, and possible wildlife.
Morning
Schwabacher Landing, then Mormon Row Historic District and the Moulton Barn.
Midday
Jenny Lake: shore walk or boat to the Hidden Falls trail, depending on time and parking.
Afternoon
Continue north on the John D. Rockefeller Jr. Memorial Parkway to Flagg Ranch.
Oxbow BendSchwabacher LandingMormon RowMoulton BarnJenny LakeHidden Falls (optional)Rockefeller Parkway
From the trail Oxbow Bend is best at dawn — the light is soft and the river is calm. Schwabacher Landing is just south on US-89. Mormon Row is one of the most-photographed spots in America — the Moulton Barn framed by the Teton range is extraordinary. Sequence these stops south-to-north so the drive to Flagg Ranch flows naturally.

South EntranceLewis FallsWest Thumb Geyser BasinYellowstone LakeMud VolcanoHayden ValleyArtist PointLower FallsCanyon area
From the trail West Thumb Geyser Basin is directly on your route after the South Entrance — geysers sitting at the lake edge is a genuinely strange and beautiful sight. Don't skip it. Artist Point at Canyon is the signature overlook: the Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone with its vivid orange and yellow canyon walls dropping to the Lower Falls. The rainbow in the mist is visible in morning light.

7:00 AM
Leave Fishing Bridge early — this is the big day.
Morning
Tower Fall, then Lamar Valley. Drive slowly and allow for wildlife traffic.
Midday
Mammoth Hot Springs: lower terraces and, if time permits, the upper terrace loop.
Afternoon
Norris Geyser Basin, Old Faithful, and Grand Prismatic Spring via the Fairy Falls overlook.
Evening
Optional Firehole Lake Drive, then exit through the West Entrance.
Tower FallLamar ValleyMammoth Hot SpringsNorris Geyser BasinPorcelain BasinOld FaithfulGrand Prismatic SpringFairy Falls overlookFirehole Lake Drive (optional)West Entrance
From the trail Leave Fishing Bridge by 7 AM — this is the big day. Lamar Valley bison will stop your vehicle; build buffer time. For Grand Prismatic, the Fairy Falls overlook is the classic aerial view — the boardwalk at lake level doesn't show the colors at all. Norris Geyser Basin is often skipped but is the hottest, most active area in the park: the Porcelain Basin loop is 800 meters and unmissable.
🚨This is an aggressive day that may take 10–12 or more hours with traffic, parking, and wildlife jams. Norris or Firehole Lake Drive should be treated as optional if time becomes tight.
